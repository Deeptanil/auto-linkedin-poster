                    Token Renewal & Setup Guide
                

                
            


            
            

                
                
                
            


            
            

                

                    Why Regenerate? LinkedIn OAuth tokens expire every 60 days. The app warns you on Discord 14 and 7 days before expiry. Renewal takes under 1 minute!
                


                

                    
Step 1: Open Token Manager

                    

                        Click LinkedIn Token in the header bar (or click status under System Status).
                    

                


                

                    
Step 2: Launch OAuth Flow

                    

                        Confirm your Client ID and Client Secret, then click Launch OAuth & Generate Token.
                    

                


                

                    
Step 3: Approve in LinkedIn Browser

                    

                        Your browser opens to LinkedIn's authorization page. Click Allow.
                    

                


                

                    
Step 4: Update GitHub Secrets

                    

                        Click Copy GitHub Secrets and paste into your GitHub repo settings under Settings → Secrets → Actions.

  1. Go to LinkedIn Developer Portal.
    2. 
                    
  2. Click Create App. Enter name, associate any LinkedIn page, and upload a logo.
    3. 
                    
  3. Under Products tab, request access for Share on LinkedIn and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect.
    4. 
                    
  4. Under Auth tab, add redirect URI:
                        
    http://localhost:8765/callback
                    
    5. 
                    
  5. Copy Client ID and Client Secret into the Token Generator modal.
